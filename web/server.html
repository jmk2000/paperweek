The responsive planner uses the shared C engine for rota status. School lessons are optional; clubs and music lessons remain visible as commitments. Screen colour and pigment refresh are not calibrated to a physical e-paper device.

Enabled by default. While this app is open and connected, all dates in the server’s browsing range (roughly two years either side of today) are downloaded automatically, future months first. Only data already published by your calendar sources can be saved. Saved data lasts up to seven days, or until this device’s pairing expires if sooner. It is unencrypted browser storage. Online revocation clears it when the device next connects; a fully offline device cannot receive revocation.
